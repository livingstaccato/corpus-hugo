**Does this theme require the hub switcher?** No — `knowledge_id` and
`knowledge_hub_root` are optional. A standalone site just gets a plain
title link and no dropdown.

**Does search require a separate index build?** Yes — `search-index.json`
is generated by whatever builds your site's content (this exampleSite
ships a minimal one in `exampleSite/layouts/`). The theme only ships the
client-side search UI.
